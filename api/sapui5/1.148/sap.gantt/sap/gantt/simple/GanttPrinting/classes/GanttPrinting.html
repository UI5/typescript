Class: GanttPrinting

import GanttPrinting from "sap/gantt/simple/GanttPrinting";

The GanttPrinting control enables you to export your Gantt chart as a PDF document. Please note that the quality of the exported image in PDF is currently limited to a pixel density of 96 PPI and the area of the chart that can be exported is limited to a canvas area of 16,384 x 16,384 pixels (268,435,456 square pixels).
